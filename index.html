My Poems 🔥 10

THE COFFEE WE SHARE

I wake before the kettle sings your name. You steal my mug and never put it down.

HOW YOU FOLD MY SHIRTS

You fold my shirts like you mean it.

YOUR KEYS ON MY TABLE

Your keys land loud on my table.

GROCERY LIST LOVE

We write grocery list on back of bills.

WE COOK UGLY RICE

We cook rice that always sticks.

THE DISHES AT MIDNIGHT

We do dishes at midnight again.

NIGHT BUS

Your head finds my shoulder.

YOUR UMBRELLA IS TOO SMALL

We both get wet, but never let go.

HOW YOU STEAL MY HOODIE

You steal my hoodie every morning.

THE WAY YOU CHARGE MY PHONE

You charge my chaos with your calm.
