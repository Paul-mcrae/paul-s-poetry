PAUL

KYOTERA POET • READER IN THE FIELD

I am Paul, 20 years, Kyotera Uganda. Real Ugandan love poems - coffee, shirts, keys, ugly rice. Reader in the field.

My Poems 🔥 10

THE COFFEE WE SHARE

I wake before the kettle sings your name, You shuffle in, your hair a tangled crown.

HOW YOU FOLD MY SHIRTS

You fold my shirts like you mean it, Not perfect, but with care.

YOUR KEYS ON MY TABLE

Your keys land loud on my table, A jingle that means you are home.

GROCERY LIST LOVE

We write our grocery list on the back of bills.

WE COOK UGLY RICE

We cook rice that always sticks a bit, But perfect evenings we have.

THE DISHES AT MIDNIGHT

We do dishes at midnight again.

NIGHT BUS

Your head finds my shoulder, heavy, yet.

YOUR UMBRELLA IS TOO SMALL

We both get wet, but never let go.

HOW YOU STEAL MY HOODIE

You steal my hoodie every cold morning.

THE WAY YOU CHARGE MY PHONE

My phone dies always at eleven percent, You charge my chaos with your calm.
