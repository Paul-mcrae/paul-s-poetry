PAUL

KYOTERA POET • READER IN THE FIELD

I am Paul, Kyotera Uganda. Real Ugandan love poems.

If I had a heart to take with a chance to be loved by you, I'd consider coming home, before I'm reminded of the 1,859 miles one of us would have to walk, and what could happen if we were just in a room to talk. Like me in your clothes eating breakfast, you in my bed fast asleep... You were beautiful, funny, thoughtful, your eyes weren't hungry, they pleaded to be gentle. So if I had a heart to take, you wouldn't need to convince it, for if you have already stolen something, you cannot steal it again.
