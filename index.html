PAUL KAGANDA

I am Paul Kaganda, 20 years, Kyotera Uganda. I write about love in small things - coffee, shirts, keys, ugly rice. Real Ugandan love.

My Poems 🔥

THE COFFEE WE SHARE

I wake before the kettle sings your name, The kitchen cold without your sleepy light.

HOW YOU FOLD MY SHIRTS

You fold my shirts like you mean it, Not perfect, but with care.

YOUR KEYS ON MY TABLE

Your keys land loud on my table, A jingle that means you are home.

GROCERY LIST LOVE

We write our grocery list on the back of bills.

WE COOK UGLY RICE

We cook rice that always sticks a bit.

THE DISHES AT MIDNIGHT

We do dishes at midnight again.

NIGHT BUS YOUR HEAD

The night bus smells of diesel and day-old sweat.

YOUR UMBRELLA IS TOO SMALL

Your umbrella is too small for two.

HOW YOU STEAL MY HOODIE

You steal my hoodie every cold morning.

THE WAY YOU CHARGE MY PHONE

My phone dies always at eleven percent.
